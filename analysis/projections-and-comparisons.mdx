---
title: "Projections and comparisons"
description: "Change the population year and choose what the catchment is compared with."
---

Use the controls at the top of the analysis workspace to change the time period and reference shown alongside your catchment.

## Change the projection year

1. Select the current **Projection year**.
2. Use the slider or step buttons to choose a year.
3. Check the updated population figures and charts.

The selected year applies to projected population measures. Measures tied to a fixed source year keep their own date.

## Choose a comparison

Open **Comparison**, then choose one of two modes:

- **Area** compares the catchment with an available reference such as its local authority, county, region, national baseline or a configured catchment model.
- **Target** compares the catchment with another selected location.

Only comparisons with data available for the current site and analysis are shown.

<Tip>
  State the projection year and comparison when sharing a figure. Both can materially change how the result should be read.
</Tip>

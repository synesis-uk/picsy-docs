---
title: "Export an analysis"
description: "Create a printable report or download an Excel workbook."
---

Open **Export** at the top of a site's analysis workspace. The available options are **Print report** and **Excel workbook**.

## Save a PDF report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the analysis">
    Choose the view, catchment, projection year, comparison and map style you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    Select **Export**, then **Print report**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it as a PDF">
    In the browser print dialog, choose **Save as PDF**, check the preview and save the file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Download an Excel workbook

Select **Export**, then **Excel workbook**. PICSy prepares the workbook and downloads it when it is ready.

The workbook contains the available data for the exported analysis, organised into worksheets for further review and calculation.

<Note>
  Large workbooks can take a little longer to prepare. Keep the analysis page open until the download starts.
</Note>
